      Box cannot find or join a network?

      The box only works with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Most networks offer it, but some phones and routers switch it off.

      • iPhone hotspot: Settings, Personal Hotspot. Turn on Allow Others to Join and Maximize Compatibility (that is the 2.4 GHz switch). Keep that screen open while the box joins: iPhones stop advertising the hotspot after a minute or two otherwise.
      • Android hotspot: Settings, Hotspot and tethering, Wi-Fi hotspot. Set the band (AP band) to 2.4 GHz, and the security to WPA2.
      • Home or venue router: make sure the 2.4 GHz band is switched on. If the router shows one name for both bands and the box still cannot join, give the 2.4 GHz band its own name in the router's settings and join that.
      • WPA3 only: set the network to WPA2, or WPA2/WPA3 mixed.
      • Hotel, cafe or airport Wi-Fi with a sign-in page will not work. Use a phone hotspot instead.
      • Passwords are case sensitive, and must be 8 to 63 characters. Open networks need none.
      • Box joined your phone's hotspot but lakehorseradio.local will not open? On an iPhone hotspot try 172.20.10.2, then .3, .4 and so on. The box is usually one of the first few addresses.

      Tip: add your phone's hotspot while you are at home. When you take the box out, turn the hotspot on and the box finds it by itself.
