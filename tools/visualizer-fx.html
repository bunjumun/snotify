Visualizer FX Settings

Effects
Each slider is the resting value; the thin bar under it is where the sound and the sticks have actually pushed it. Choosing an effect by hand pauses the auto-cycle.
Camera layer
Camera is off.
Hold LT (or Q) to dissolve to the camera; let go and it springs back to the resting mix. The first pull asks for camera access. The picture is processed on this page and never uploaded.
Mix
Tap in time to set the pulse's tempo, with or without audio.
Decks
The six mixer scenes (Tunnel, Plasma, Stars, Waves, Grid, Vortex) follow the sticks as position, twist and zoom. The five Visualizer scenes take every parameter and every audio route.
Audio Input
Not connected: it drifts on its own until you play it.
Device names stay hidden until you grant mic access once — hit Connect, allow, then pick your real device and connect again. Route a DAW bus in through BlackHole, Loopback, or any virtual audio device to drive this per channel.
Live Channels
Solo a track in your DAW to find which channel it lands on.
Modulation Routing
The party map is already loaded: bass pushes the effect's amount, warp and threshold and swells the scene; mids breathe the scale and complexity; highs drive the RGB and CMYK shift and the glow. Mix means the loudest channel, so it works on any input. A negative depth pulls a parameter down instead of up.
Response
Pulse is how hard the sound hits the picture: 0 ignores it, 1.5 lets it throw everything about. Demo pulse is an optional stand-in for audio, so you can see what the map does with nothing connected; it is off by default and with nothing playing the tool is ambient. Auto level learns each band's own floor and peak, so the same mapping works from a quiet mic or a hot DAW bus. Idle motion sets how alive it is with no input at all. Beat threshold is how far the bass must jump above its own average to count as a beat: higher means fewer beats.
MIDI
MIDI is off.
Click a row, then move a knob or press a pad. Assignments save to this browser. MIDI is only requested once you enable it, so the browser does not ask on every visit.
