Lakehorse Swimulator

Ride the kelpie through the wreck of the Starship Enias. You're only the diver holding on. Tap for a tail beat, hold to boost, and every beat costs you air. The record plays while you swim.

Jupiter Gold

The interactive music video. A five-minute climate story drawn over a real map, timed to the song. Pick Jupiter Gold on the first screen to start it. Live-data layers (satellites, launches, aircraft) need a server and stay empty here.
